# ScopePreviewList

The scope preview before a center question: every record that will be read with author, time, audience and trust class; what was omitted and why; payer and cost ceiling; the audience.
This is what makes the center auditable, and it is the interface's answer to the audience rule: a general-room question that needs project records is moved, not answered.

## States

Complete; partial coverage stated as a consequence; budget cap reached; general-room audience restriction with the move offered.

## Do not

- Do not summarise the list as a count.
- Do not let the preview appear after the answer.
