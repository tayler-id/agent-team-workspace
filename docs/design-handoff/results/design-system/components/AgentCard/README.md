# AgentCard
The agent session card in the timeline: one center question or one run's typed activity, from queued to committed.
It is a neutral tile with a hairline. The agent's identity is the small pastel square in the kicker; nothing else is coloured. Provisional streaming text is `ink-muted` and labelled, and is replaced by the committed answer, never merged with it.
## States
Queued within 10 seconds; streaming provisional; committed with citations and a partial-coverage chip when records were omitted; a question from a run with its deadline and an Answer control; unresponsive after 10 seconds without acknowledgment; error with reason code, what to do and the correlation ID; insufficient evidence stated plainly.
## Do
- Show cost and payer on the kicker. Whose account is charged is never implied.
- Link only citations generated from stored IDs.
- Offer the move to the project room on a scope error rather than answering with a leak.
## Do not
- Do not write that the agent thinks or believes. It reported.
- Do not let a card's last sentence stand in for run state.
