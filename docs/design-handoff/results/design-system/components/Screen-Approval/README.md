# Screen-Approval
Steps 10 and 11: approving publication and seeing the draft open.
The approval pane is a contract: the action with head and base, what it lets happen, what it is bound to, what it does not do, and why the reader may decide, with recent-authentication age. The grant button binds the head. In the timeline the publication card shows granted by whom, the owner's credential confirmation, the read-back receipt, and `Draft open · CI pending` as a live chip until required checks pass.
