# SystemEvent
A flat timeline line recording a membership change, a redaction, a resync or a revocation: grey mark, one sentence naming actor and effect, a timestamp.
It carries no control. Acting on membership happens in the member controls with a preview and a receipt.
## Do
- Name the actor and the effect. "Ada revoked Ben", not "Member removed".
- Enumerate what a revocation ended, including what cannot be undone.
## Do not
- Do not attach a button, undo or link.
- Do not colour a destructive fact.
