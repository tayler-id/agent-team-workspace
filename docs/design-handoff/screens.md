# Screen and surface inventory

Every surface the [priority workflows](workflows.md) need, with its purpose, primary data, actions, required states, responsive behavior, and stage. Sources: spec [R02](../technical-spec.md#r02-browser-experience-and-user-journeys), [R06](../technical-spec.md#r06-state-machines-and-invariants), [R11](../technical-spec.md#r11-git-evidence-publication-review-and-merge), [R18](../technical-spec.md#r18-accessibility-and-performance-budgets), [R23](../technical-spec.md#r23-selected-browser-renderer-qualification).

Design targets are 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 phone. On the phone the right pane becomes a route; "collapses" below means how that route stacks. Every surface needs empty, loading, error, revoked, and offline states; the table names only the states that differ from the defaults in [interaction and state](interaction-and-state.md).

## First prototype

The smallest coherent prototype is the row set labelled `S1 prototype`: sign-in and invitation, the shell, a room timeline with thread pane and composer, the scope preview and agent session card, the task editor and card, the launch preview and run card and pane, the recovery sheet, the evidence pane, the approval card, publication status, and the inbox. Together they carry one two-person story: invite, talk, ask, create, launch, follow, recover, review, approve. Design that story end to end on both viewports before widening to search, machines, settings, and S2 surfaces.
